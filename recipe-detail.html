Italian

Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Prep Time: 10 min | Cook Time: 30 min | Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup chicken broth
  • ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Sear in a skillet until golden. Remove and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, sauté garlic, then add cream, broth, and sun-dried tomatoes.
  3. Return chicken to pan and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add spinach and Parmesan. Cook until spinach wilts.

Indian

Classic Butter Chicken

Prep Time: 20 min | Cook Time: 25 min | Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and spices for 1 hour.
  2. Cook onions, garlic, and ginger in butter until golden.
  3. Add spices and tomato sauce. Simmer, then add chicken and cream.
  4. Cook gently for 15 minutes until chicken is tender.

Mexican

Chicken Fajitas

Prep Time: 15 min | Cook Time: 15 min | Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken strips
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Tortillas, sour cream, guacamole

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken with lime juice and seasoning.
  2. Sauté chicken until cooked. Remove.
  3. Cook peppers and onions until charred.
  4. Serve with warm tortillas and toppings.

Thai

Thai Basil Beef (Pad Kra Pao)

Prep Time: 10 min | Cook Time: 15 min | Servings: 3

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Thai chilies
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Stir-fry garlic and chilies in oil.
  2. Add beef and cook until browned.
  3. Season with sauces and finish with fresh basil.

Japanese

Teriyaki Salmon Bowl

Prep Time: 10 min | Cook Time: 15 min | Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated

Instructions

  1. Mix sauce ingredients and marinate salmon for 10 minutes.
  2. Pan-sear salmon skin-side down, then brush with sauce.
  3. Serve over rice with steamed vegetables.

French

Classic Coq au Vin

Prep Time: 30 min | Cook Time: 60 min | Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 8 oz bacon, diced
  • 12 pearl onions
  • 8 oz mushrooms
  • 2 carrots, chopped

Instructions

  1. Brown chicken and bacon. Remove.
  2. Sauté vegetables, then return chicken and add wine.
  3. Simmer covered for 45–60 minutes until tender.
